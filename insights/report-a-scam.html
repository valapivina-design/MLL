How to report a scam

Knowing how to report a scam means finding the right agency, assembling a clear evidence pack, and keeping a proper record — not venting on social media.

Report a scam — where to start

To report a scam, match the channel to the money path. Card payments → issuer dispute. Bank wire → bank fraud desk quickly. Crypto → local cybercrime unit plus any exchange that still holds funds. Romance or WhatsApp funnels → preserve chats before reporting.

How to report a scam in practice: write a one-page timeline, attach tx IDs or wire refs, list every domain and phone number, and keep originals. Then file with the bank/card network, national fraud portals where you live, and — for investment pitches — the securities or financial regulator that covers that product.

Report a scam website

When you report a scam website, capture the exact URL, WHOIS if available, screenshots of the pitch, and the deposit instructions. Hosting providers and registrars sometimes act on abuse reports; regulators track clones of licensed brands. Cross-check our Company & Broker Index if the name already appears in public complaint patterns.

Report crypto scam / report online trading scam

  • Report crypto scam: include wallet addresses, tx hashes, and the fake support channel.
  • Report online trading scam: include the broker domain, account IDs, and every “fee to withdraw” demand.
Cited practice point: Keep your filing reference numbers. Banks, card networks, and regulators often ask for the same evidence pack later — timeline, domains, and payment proofs in one place.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on reporting a scam, recovery options, and when a confidential consultation may help.

How do I report a scam?

Document the timeline and payment proofs, notify your bank or card issuer immediately, then file with the appropriate fraud/cyber unit and any financial regulator that covers the product. Keep copies of every filing reference number.

How do I report a scam website?

Save the full URL, screenshots, and payment instructions, then submit abuse reports to the host/registrar where possible and include the same pack in your police or regulator complaint.

Should I report a crypto scam if the coins already moved?

Yes. Reports create a record, help pattern databases, and can still matter if funds later hit a cooperative exchange. Delay makes freezes harder.
