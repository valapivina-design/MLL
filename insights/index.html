Insights FAQ

Short answers before you open a folder or check the broker index.

What is this Insights section for?

It explains common fraud patterns — crypto scams, scam wallets, Ponzi schemes, WhatsApp pig butchering — and the difference between reporting, tracing, and litigation. Use it alongside the Company & Broker Index when a platform name is already on file.

Do you guarantee crypto scam recovery?

No. Pages on recover stolen crypto and forex scam recovery describe pathways and risks — banks, exchanges, reports, and civil options where the facts support them. Nothing here promises a full return of funds.

Where should I start if I need to report a scam today?

Begin with How to report a scam, notify your bank or card issuer, preserve chats and transaction IDs, then request a consultation if the loss is material.
