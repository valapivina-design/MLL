Privacy Policy

How Mike Levy Law collects, uses, and protects information you submit through this website.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

Mike Levy Law (“MLL”, “we”, “us”) operates mikelevylaw.com and related pages, including the Company & Broker Index and Insights. Our contact details are: 1395 Brickell Ave, Suite 800, Miami, FL 33131, USA · info@mikelevylaw.com.

Information we collect

We may collect information you submit through forms on this site, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Company or broker name, matter details, and free-text comments or case descriptions
  • Technical data such as page URL, approximate location inferred from your connection, and basic device or browser information

We do not ask you to send passwords, seed phrases, or full payment-card numbers through public forms. Do not include highly sensitive financial credentials in a comment or intake form.

How we use information

  • To respond to consultation requests and moderate public comments
  • To evaluate whether a matter may warrant further review
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website
  • To comply with legal obligations

Submitting a form does not create an attorney–client relationship. We decide which comments, if any, are published after review. Contact details you provide on comment forms are not published.

Sharing

Form submissions may be processed by our form provider (FormCarry) acting on our instructions. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or with service providers bound to keep it confidential.

Retention

We retain intake and comment records for as long as needed to respond, maintain records of inquiries, meet legal requirements, or resolve disputes, then delete or anonymize them when no longer required.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and our professional obligations. Contact info@mikelevylaw.com. If you are in a jurisdiction with additional privacy rights (for example certain U.S. states or the EEA/UK), you may also have rights to restrict processing or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International visitors

Our firm is based in the United States. If you contact us from outside the U.S., your information may be processed in the United States.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you have reviewed the revised policy.

Contact

Privacy questions: info@mikelevylaw.com · Mike Levy Law · 1395 Brickell Ave, Suite 800 · Miami, FL 33131 · USA.
